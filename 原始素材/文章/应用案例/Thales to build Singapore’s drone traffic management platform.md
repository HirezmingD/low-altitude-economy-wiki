- The Civil Aviation Authority of Singapore (CAAS) has awarded Thales, as the leading partner in a consortium with Deeeplabs, a contract to deliver an Unmanned Aircraft Systems (UAS) Traffic Management (UTM) System based on the TopSky - AstraUTM platform, to provide an integrated, one-stop portal for the approval and tracking of all unmanned aircraft (UA) operations in Singapore.  
	新加坡民航局 (CAAS) 已授予泰雷兹公司（Thales）与 Deeeplabs 组成的联合体中的牵头合作伙伴一份合同，以交付基于 TopSky - AstraUTM 平台的无人机系统 (UAS) 交通管理 (UTM) 系统，为新加坡所有无人机 (UA) 运行的审批和跟踪提供集成的一站式门户。
- The solution will combine Thales’ aviation expertise with strong local digital and cloud capabilities to support the development of Singapore’s national drone ecosystem.  
	该解决方案将结合泰雷兹的航空专业知识和强大的本地数字及云能力，以支持新加坡国家无人机生态系统的发展。

Under this contract, Thales will deploy a new UTM system, based on its TopSky - AstraUTM solution, in Singapore, a cutting-edge digital platform designed to enable the secure and scalable management of unmanned aircraft operations. This system will provide CAAS with a single platform with streamlined application processes, improved regulatory approvals processing time and enhanced situational awareness to support the growing number of drone operations while maintaining the highest levels of aviation safety.  
根据这份合同，泰雷兹将在新加坡部署一套基于其 TopSky-AstraUTM 解决方案的新型无人机交通管理（UTM）系统。该系统是一个尖端的数字平台，旨在实现对无人机运行的安全且可扩展的管理。该系统将为新加坡民航局（CAAS）提供一个统一的平台，简化申请流程，缩短监管审批时间，并增强态势感知能力，从而在确保最高航空安全水平的同时，支持日益增长的无人机运行需求。

TopSky - AstraUTM integrates advanced flight management with digital regulatory services to support the entire lifecycle of drone operations. This platform enables key functions such as operator and drone registration, digital flight planning and authorisation, and real-time monitoring of drone operations. By enhancing situational awareness and automation, the system ensures efficient coordination of unmanned flights across Singapore’s airspace.  
TopSky - AstraUTM 将先进的飞行管理与数字化监管服务相结合，为无人机运营的整个生命周期提供支持。该平台具备关键功能，例如操作员和无人机注册、数字化飞行计划和授权，以及无人机运营的实时监控。通过增强态势感知和自动化，该系统确保了新加坡空域内无人机飞行的高效协调。

Thales will deliver the project as part of a consortium with Deeeplabs, a Singapore-based technology provider, which will support deployment and operational services. This collaboration combines global aviation expertise with strong local digital capabilities to support Singapore’s innovation and smart mobility goals.  
泰雷兹将与总部位于新加坡的技术提供商 Deeeplabs 组成联合体来交付该项目，Deeeplabs 将提供部署和运营服务。此次合作将全球航空领域的专业知识与强大的本地数字化能力相结合，以支持新加坡的创新和智能交通目标。

The UTM system will play a key role in Singapore’s unmanned aircraft industry, supporting industries including logistics, infrastructure inspection, maritime operations, emergency response, and urban mobility. This contract further reinforces Thales’ leadership in Unmanned Air Traffic Management and Advanced Air Mobility solutions, following the integration of AstraUTM into its portfolio in 2024 and its subsequent deployment across multiple countries.  
UTM 系统将在新加坡的无人机产业中发挥关键作用，为物流、基础设施巡检、海事作业、应急响应和城市交通等行业提供支持。继 AstraUTM 系统于 2024 年整合到泰雷兹的产品组合并在多个国家部署之后，这份合同进一步巩固了泰雷兹在无人机交通管理和先进空中交通解决方案领域的领先地位。

**About Thales 关于泰雷兹**

Thales (Euronext Paris: HO) is a global leader in advanced technologies for the Defence, Aerospace, and Cyber & Digital sectors. Its portfolio of innovative products and services helps address several major challenges: sovereignty, security, sustainability and inclusion.  
泰雷兹（巴黎泛欧交易所：HO）是国防、航空航天以及网络与数字领域先进技术的全球领导者。其创新产品和服务组合有助于应对主权、安全、可持续性和包容性等几项重大挑战。

The Group allocates €4.5 billion per year in Research & Development in key areas, particularly for critical environments, such as Artificial Intelligence, Cybersecurity, Quantum and Cloud technologies.  
该集团每年投入 45 亿欧元用于关键领域的研发，特别是人工智能、网络安全、量子和云计算等关键环境。

Thales has more than 85,000 employees in 65 countries. In 2025, the Group generated sales of €22.1 billion.

**About Thales in Singapore**

Thales established its presence in Singapore in 1973 to support the growth of aerospace activities in Asia. Since then, it has grown to be a leading deep-tech company operating in the Aeronautics (including avionics and air traffic management), Defence, Public Security, Cybersecurity & Digital Identity sectors. Thales in Singapore runs global industrial operations for avionics and digital identity solutions and has a strong commitment to Research, Technology and Innovation, with Centres of Excellence for avionics, defence, inflight entertainment, naval drones, public security, radars and space. With over 2000 employees across four locations, Thales is actively supporting Singapore in driving its digital transformation and Smart Nation ambitions.  
泰雷兹于1973年在新加坡设立分支机构，旨在支持亚洲航空航天业的发展。自那时起，泰雷兹已发展成为一家领先的深科技公司，业务涵盖航空（包括航空电子设备和空中交通管理）、国防、公共安全、网络安全和数字身份等领域。泰雷兹新加坡公司负责全球航空电子设备和数字身份解决方案的工业运营，并致力于研发和创新，设有航空电子设备、国防、机载娱乐、海军无人机、公共安全、雷达和航天等领域的卓越中心。泰雷兹在新加坡的四个地点拥有超过2000名员工，积极支持新加坡推进数字化转型和智慧国家建设。
